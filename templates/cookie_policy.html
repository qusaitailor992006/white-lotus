Privacy & Tracking Safeguards

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Saras Baug Road, Pune

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to make web portals operate smoothly, remember user preferences, and secure administrative sessions.

2. How White Lotus Uses Storage (Zero Ad Trackers)

We use strictly necessary and functional cookies only:

  • Essential Staff Session Cookie (whitelotus_admin_session): Used exclusively for floor staff and management authentication into the Admin Command Dashboard. Configured with HttpOnly; SameSite=Strict for maximum protection.
  • Cookie Consent Preference (wl_cookie_consent): Stored locally in your browser to remember that you acknowledged our privacy standards so the notice is not shown repeatedly.
✓ No Cross-Site Advertising Cookies · No Facebook Pixel · No Invasive Telemetry

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Doing so will not affect your ability to browse our menu or reserve dining tables.
