Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 & IT Act 2000

1. Overview & Commitment

White Lotus: Food Vibe Folks ("we", "our", "us"), situated at 202, 203 Saras Baug Road, Pune 411009, values your privacy and personal autonomy. This Privacy Policy details our transparent data collection, utilization, and protective protocols for guests reserving tables or inquiring about banquet facilities through our platform.

2. Data We Collect (Strict Data Minimization)

We collect only the bare minimum data necessary to allocate your table and provide bespoke dining service:

  • Identity & Contact: Full Name and Mobile Number (used strictly for WhatsApp reservation confirmation and floor coordination).
  • Dining Preferences: Date of reservation, selected time slot, party size, preferred dining sanctuary (Terrace Garden or AC Lounge), and dietary or celebration notes provided voluntarily by you.
  • Corporate & Banquet Data: Contact email and guest counts for events exceeding 20 attendees.
🔒 What We NEVER Collect: We do not collect, process, or store payment card numbers, UPI PINs, Aadhaar numbers, biometric data, or passwords of public dining guests.

3. Purpose of Processing & Zero Third-Party Selling

Your personal data is utilized solely for:

  • Confirming table reservations and transmitting real-time floor updates via WhatsApp.
  • Reserving on-site dedicated car parking slots at our Saras Baug Road location.
  • Tailoring dietary requirements (such as Jain, vegan, or allergy accommodations).

We have never sold, rented, or commercialized diner data, and we never will. Your data is stored on private servers and is never shared with third-party advertising networks.

4. Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023 & Data Erasure

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the absolute right to access, rectify, or demand the permanent deletion of your customer profile and booking records.

How to Request Immediate Data Purge:

You may submit a deletion request via our one-click portal on the homepage or email our privacy desk directly at concierge@whitelotuspune.com with your phone number. Your record will be permanently purged within 24 hours.

5. Data Security Standards

Our systems enforce industry-standard security safeguards: encrypted SSL/TLS data transmission, strict HTTP security headers, parameterized database queries preventing SQL injection, and administrative access protected by bcrypt-hashed authentication.
