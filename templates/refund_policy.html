Consumer Transparency

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Saras Baug Road, Pune

1. Zero Hidden Fees Guarantee

At White Lotus, we believe in complete commercial transparency:

  • Table Reservations are 100% Free: There are zero booking fees, platform surcharges, or upfront deposits required to reserve a table.
  • Transparent In-House Billing: Food, beverage, and bar items are billed strictly at the prices listed on our official menu. Applicable GST and service charges are clearly displayed on your final printed tax invoice.

2. Table Reservation Cancellations

If your plans change, you may cancel or reschedule your reservation at zero cost. We kindly ask that you send a brief message to the Floor Captain on WhatsApp at +91 95135 75757 at least 1 hour prior to your reserved slot so the table can be extended to waiting diners.

3. Banquet & Private Event Advances (20–150 Pax)

For large private group banquets requiring dedicated sections of the Starlit Terrace or AC Lounge:

  • Rescheduling Flexibility: If notified at least 7 days before the event, 100% of the advance deposit can be adjusted toward any alternate date within 90 days.
  • Cancellations: In cases of unavoidable cancellations requested at least 10 days in advance, 80% of the booking deposit is refundable, with 20% retained to cover perishable procurement and dedicated staffing arrangements.

4. Contact Desk

For any billing questions or banquet adjustments, please contact our management team directly at +91 95135 75757 or email concierge@whitelotuspune.com.
