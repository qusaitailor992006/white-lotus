Table reservations made through our direct web portal or WhatsApp desk are subject to floor availability. We honor a 15-minute grace period from your confirmed reservation time. In the event of an unavoidable delay, please notify the Floor Captain at +91 95135 75757 so we can protect your table.

2. Dining Sanctuaries & Seating Allocation

Guests may specify a sanctuary preference (The Starlit Terrace Garden or The Imperial Velvet AC Lounge). While we endeavor to seat every party in their requested zone, table allocations are optimized based on party size, weather considerations (for open-air terrace seating), and operational flow.

White Lotus provides 10 to 15 on-site dedicated car parking slots with valet attendant assistance directly outside our entrance on Saras Baug Road. Parking is provided complimentary for dining patrons. Vehicles and belongings are parked at the owner's risk.
